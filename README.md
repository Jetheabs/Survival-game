# Game-Jam
Survival harvesting game using Godot (Inspired by the oreagon trail)

-As you travel across different areas collecting materials to bring along your journey, you realize that the path you are taking is more dangerous than expected.
-On your path their are villages or towns you can trade with as you proceed in your journey to survive creatures that reside on your path.

-The game assets and characters are made with pixel art.
